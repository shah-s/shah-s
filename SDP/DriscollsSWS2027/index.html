Questions

Good to know

Do I need to share to play the berry spin?

No. Every eligible registrant gets the instant win berry spin immediately after signing up. Sharing only earns additional sweepstakes entries.

What counts as a successful referral?

An eligible, validated friend registration through your unique link. Copying or sending your link counts as a share, and you earn a referral entry once your friend signs up.

Why do you ask for my mailing address?

So we can ship prizes to winners and confirm eligibility. Your address is used only to administer this promotion.

Is there a limit on bonus entries?

Yes. Proposed limits on social share entries and referral entries will be set in the Official Rules.

Can I refer myself or use a second email?

No. Self referrals and duplicate registrations are not eligible and are screened by the platform.

Where are the Official Rules?

Final Official Rules will be published at launch. This demo uses placeholder copy.
